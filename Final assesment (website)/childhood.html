My childhood was filled with love and affection from my family and friends.

Childhood and my upbringing was different and interesting. I wasnt the nicest kid when I was younger... But a lot of kids are assholes so not much to note. My youth was filled with visits to people I didnt know to get me diagnosed, visits to therapists to help my depression once I entered highschool.

Despite these visits, my childhood was filled with laughter and joy. My creativity blooming everyday, writing stories and drawing out wild ideas.

My cousins and my grandparents were a big part of my life and they helped shape who I am. They encouraged me and loved me regardless of the difficulties I struggled with (my adhd, insomnia, etc).

I was a nerdy kid that played video games on the weekends and drew animals in class.

A big part of my childhood was travelling with my family. My parents would take me and my sister to cuba, florida, South Carolina and other places. We would go with family friends once every 2 years!

But my childhood then shattered by the reality of the world around me: I was weird... weird kids dont fit in.. they get bullied.

I was first introduced to bullying in 6th grade when the kids behind me threw slime in my hair and pelted rocks at me. They did it because I was weird. The bullying and shame persisted into highschool, thus me going to therapy. Between the teasing and whispers from the other kids, my loneliness consumed me. Highschool was a very rough part of my life (see more in struggles)
