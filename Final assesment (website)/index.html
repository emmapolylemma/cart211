My private information isn't yours to share.

No matter what app i use or where i go, the internet knows about me and my interests... and its annoying. After reading Zuboff's text, i realized that platforms like google use my information and sell it to other platforms. What a shame, i hate that! I dont really know what to do other than deny cookies on platforms. I thought to myself, "They cant sell 'private' information is the information is out there".

This website is dedicated to giving out information about myself so my data cannot be sold. While it might be small or dangerous, I honestly don't care

In this website, I will be talking about myself, duh.
